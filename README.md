## About me

Imaging science student at RIT. I'm a programmer and an avid 3D modeler and printer, and I spend a lot of time on electronics and mechanical design. Most of what I build sits where cameras, color, and hardware meet.

## Currently working on

- [stackforge](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/stackforge): flat full-colour prints from per-pixel filament stacks on a four-toolhead printer
- [Digital-Twins-of-Artifacts](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/Digital-Twins-of-Artifacts): a system for creating 3D models and replicas of historical artifacts
- [calibration-suite](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/calibration-suite): camera sensor, lens and display calibration with provenance on every number

## Other projects

- [vision-psychophysics](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/vision-psychophysics): a local, calibrated visual psychophysics test suite
- [notes_app](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/notes_app): self-hosted notes with transcription, a keyword graph, and RAG chat over your own notes
- [discord_watch](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/discord_watch): a local model flags the Discord messages worth your attention
- [wikipedia-experiments](https://github.com/LuckyStrix/wikipedia-experiments): experiments with a full offline copy of English Wikipedia

## Tools and skills

- **Languages:** Python, C/C++, TypeScript
- **Self-hosted stack:** FastAPI, SQLite, Docker
- **Hardware:** Raspberry Pi, Arduino
- **Imaging and color science:** camera and display calibration, ICC and DCP profiles, psychophysics, OCR
